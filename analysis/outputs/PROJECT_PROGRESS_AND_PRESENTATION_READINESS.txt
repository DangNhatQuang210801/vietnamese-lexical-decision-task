5. PRELIMINARY FINDINGS

Frequency effect:
Descriptively, high-frequency words are faster than low-frequency words. Mean RTs in the current RT table are:
low = 0.668 s
mid = 0.618 s
high = 0.510 s

The model comparison indicates that adding frequency_group improves model fit compared with the baseline model.

Syllable length effect:
Descriptively, RT increases with syllable length. Mean RTs are:
1 syllable = 0.441 s
2 syllables = 0.528 s
3 syllables = 0.684 s
4 syllables = 0.748 s

The model comparison indicates that adding syllable_length strongly improves model fit.

Interaction effect:
The interaction model improves over the additive model, but the improvement is modest. The model comparison for model3 gives p = 0.0449. This should be presented cautiously as evidence that the frequency effect may vary by syllable length, not as a large or final conclusion.

Accuracy and task quality:
Accuracy suggests that participants generally performed the lexical decision task seriously. Accuracy by condition is:

pseudoword = 82.0%
