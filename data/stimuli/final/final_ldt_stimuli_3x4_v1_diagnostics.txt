9. Why frequency groups were defined within each length group
- In the full real-word pool, syllable_length and log_frequency are strongly negatively related: shorter words tend to be more frequent and longer words tend to be less frequent.
- Global tertiles would leave several frequency_group x syllable_length cells underfilled or empty.
- Within-length tertiles create high/mid/low frequency groups separately inside each syllable length, allowing a balanced 3 x 4 design with 6 real words per cell.
- This makes frequency and syllable length easier to separate experimentally, while raw frequency and log_frequency remain available for continuous analyses.
