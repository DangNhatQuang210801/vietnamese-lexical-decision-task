Generation method:
A conservative Vietnamese onset-rime inventory was used to generate pronounceable-looking syllables. Candidate syllables were built primarily from simple Vietnamese rimes with Vietnamese-specific diacritics/letters to avoid foreign-looking forms and questionable tone placement, then recombined into 1-, 2-, 3-, and 4-syllable pseudowords. Candidates were screened for Vietnamese spelling constraints, offensive/near-profane fragments, duplicate generated forms, and exact matches against processed frequency tables, the frozen real-word stimuli, and existing candidate files.

Warnings and limitations:
- This is a reviewable candidate pool, not the final pseudoword set.
- Automatic generation checks exact corpus/candidate matches only; rare real words absent from the corpus may remain.
- Pronounceability and naturalness require manual/native-speaker review.
- Multi-syllable candidates are matched by syllable_length, but not yet matched to individual real words by frequency, orthographic neighborhood, tone pattern, or other psycholinguistic controls.
- Offensive/inappropriate screening used a small obvious-fragment blocklist and should be manually reviewed.
