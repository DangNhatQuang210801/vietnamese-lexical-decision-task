How v3 differs from v2:
v2 mainly generated Vietnamese-marked syllables through onset-rime recombination, which could produce legal-looking but artificial forms. v3 uses real attested Vietnamese syllables for multi-syllable candidates and only requires the full recombined sequence to be unattested. For one-syllable candidates, v3 starts from real corpus syllables and applies small tone, vowel, or final-consonant changes.

Limitations:
- This is still a reviewable candidate pool, not a final pseudoword set.
- Meaningfulness and named-entity status are screened heuristically and need manual review.
- Exact-match filtering cannot detect rare real words absent from the input files.
- Multi-syllable candidates are not yet individually matched to real words beyond syllable length.
