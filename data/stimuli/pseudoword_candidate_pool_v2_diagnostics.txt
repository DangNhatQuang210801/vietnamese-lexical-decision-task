Generation method:
The v2 pool uses a conservative Vietnamese onset-rime inventory. Candidate syllables are built from common Vietnamese onset templates and relatively safe rime templates with Vietnamese-specific letters or tone marks. Candidates are combined into 1-, 2-, 3-, and 4-syllable forms and filtered against exact matches in processed frequency files, existing candidate pools, and the frozen final real-word stimuli.

Warnings and limitations:
- This is a reviewable candidate pool, not the final pseudoword set.
- Exact-match filtering cannot catch rare Vietnamese words absent from the input files.
- quality_flag is heuristic and should not replace manual/native-speaker review.
- Items are matched by syllable_length only; final matching still needs psycholinguistic review.
