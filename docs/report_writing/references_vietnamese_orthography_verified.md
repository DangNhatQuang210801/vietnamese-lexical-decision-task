# Vietnamese Orthography and Word Segmentation References

Scope: Vietnamese-specific sources only, covering Vietnamese orthography, Chữ Quốc Ngữ, Vietnamese syllable structure, syllable spacing, word segmentation, and Vietnamese NLP segmentation tools. Each item is marked with its verification status and source used for verification.

## Verified APA 7 References

DeFrancis, J. (1977). *Colonialism and language policy in Viet Nam*. De Gruyter Mouton. https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110802405

- Status: verified from De Gruyter Brill publisher page.
- Use for: history of language policy and the rise/officialization of Quốc Ngữ in Vietnam.
- Verification source: https://www.degruyterbrill.com/de/document/doi/10.1515/9783110802405/html

Đinh, Q. T., Lê, H. P., Nguyễn, T. M. H., Nguyễn, C. T., Rossignol, M., & Vũ, X. L. (2008). Word segmentation of Vietnamese texts: A comparison of approaches. In N. Calzolari, K. Choukri, B. Maegaard, J. Mariani, J. Odijk, S. Piperidis, & D. Tapias (Eds.), *Proceedings of the Sixth International Conference on Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC'08)*. European Language Resources Association. https://aclanthology.org/L08-1355/

- Status: verified from ACL Anthology.
- Use for: Vietnamese word segmentation; the claim that many Vietnamese words are built compositionally from syllables, creating word-boundary ambiguity; comparison of vnTokenizer, PVnSeg, and JVnSegmenter.
- Verification source: https://aclanthology.org/L08-1355/

- Status: verified from John Benjamins publisher page.
- Use for: Vietnamese grammar, phonology, writing systems, syllabic structure, morphology, and lexicon.
- Verification source: https://benjamins.com/catalog/loall.9

Nguyen, D. Q., Nguyen, D. Q., Vu, T., Dras, M., & Johnson, M. (2018). A fast and accurate Vietnamese word segmenter. In N. Calzolari, K. Choukri, C. Cieri, T. Declerck, S. Goggi, K. Hasida, H. Pham, H., & Baayen, H. (2015). Vietnamese compounds show an anti-frequency effect in visual lexical decision. *Language, Cognition and Neuroscience, 30*(9), 1077-1095. https://doi.org/10.1080/23273798.2015.1054844

- Status: verified from Taylor & Francis publisher page.
- Use for: Vietnamese bisyllabic compounds, visual lexical decision, syllabeme-based lexical processing, and corpus/psycholinguistic evidence that compounds are central to Vietnamese lexical structure.

Thompson, L. C. (1965). *A Vietnamese grammar*. University of Washington Press.

- Status: verified bibliographic details from Open Library record linked to Internet Archive, LCCN, and OCLC.
- Use for: classic descriptive grammar; Vietnamese phonology and syllable structure.
- Verification source: https://openlibrary.org/books/OL5952272M/A_Vietnamese_grammar

Verdonschot, R. G., Hoàng, T. L. P., & Tamaoka, K. (2022). Phonological encoding in Vietnamese: An experimental investigation. *Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology, 75*(7), 1289-1305. https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218211053244

- Status: verified from SAGE journal page.
- Use for: Vietnamese phonological encoding; direct statement that modern Vietnamese uses Chữ Quốc Ngữ, that tone is written with diacritics, and that compound-word syllables are still separated by spaces, creating syllable blocks in running text.
- Verification source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/17470218211053244
